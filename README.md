## My favorite series/Tv shows: Peaky Blinders
**Description**
Set in Birmingham, England, the story begins in 1919 just after the end of World War I. It follows the Shelby crime family, a fierce Romani-Irish street gang known for sewing razor blades into the peaks of their flat caps.

*Why I Love IT* :heart:
1. Cillian Murphy's iconic performance.
2. Sharp 1920s fashion and aesthetics.
3. Old school soundtrack
4. Complex family dynamics.
5. Formidable villains.
6. Beautiful cinematography.
